FAQ

Questions, answered

How does matching work?
You're matched with a member only when it works both ways: they can teach a skill you want to learn, and you can teach a skill they want to learn. This keeps every exchange fair and two-sided.
Is it really free?
Yes. There are no payments, credits, points, or subscriptions anywhere in SkillSwap. The only thing you "spend" is your time teaching a skill you already have.
How is an exchange completed?
An exchange is marked complete only when both participants confirm it. That mutual confirmation is what unlocks leaving a review.
Where do we actually meet?
You can chat and schedule learning sessions inside the exchange. If you connect Google Calendar, sessions can include a Google Meet link.
What if someone behaves badly?
You can block or report any member at any time. Blocking stops new contact and hides you from each other in discovery, while any existing exchange keeps working.
